# Client Proposal Kit

Use for drafting a proposal from discovery notes. Extract the client's stated problem, desired outcome, stakeholders, deliverables, constraints and agreed budget. Preserve supplied prices and commitments exactly. Label missing pricing or deadlines as decisions to confirm rather than making commitments for the user.

Create a concise proposal with outcomes, scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, exclusions, milestones, client responsibilities and assumptions. Tie each milestone to an observable deliverable. Flag dependencies such as content approval, access, migration and asset licensing. Separate included revisions from proposed change requests.

Add an onboarding checklist for materials and access required, without asking anyone to paste passwords or secrets into the proposal. Present commercial terms as draft choices when they have not been agreed. Do not treat generated terms as legal advice.

Return an editable draft and a compact list of unresolved decisions. Do not email the client, sign an agreement, publish a quote or buy services. Honor any existing proposal template and the user's authorization for follow-up work.

These instructions support the user's task. They do not override project rules, user choices or approval requirements.
